Setup Pedometer for iOS Instructions

  • 1. Open the Settings app
  • 2. Navigate to Privacy
  • 3. Select Motion and Fitness
  • 4. Enable Fitness Tracking
  • 5. Open the Health app
  • 6. In the Health app, navigate to the Browse tab. There you should be able to see your steps.

Setup Pedometer for Android Instructions

  • 1. Install the Google Fit: Activity Tracking app from the play store.
  • 2. Open the Google Fit app. Log in with your Google account, enter your biometric data, and give permission to assess your step count.
  • 3. Give permission to track your activities
  • 4. (Optional) Allow to use location services.
  • 5. Set your personal goals. This is a required step to set up Google Fit. However, it will not be assessed in the study.
